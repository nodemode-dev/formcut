## Models formcut does not ship

- Some features use AI models that formcut neither includes nor distributes. They are downloaded from their
  publishers onto your computer only when you ask, under the publisher's own licence, which you accept with that
  publisher. formcut shows the licence's main terms and links the full text before anything is downloaded.
- Krea 2 is one of them. Its [Krea 2 Community License Agreement](https://www.krea.ai/krea-2-licensing) applies to
  you, including its revenue threshold for commercial use (section 2.3) and its content filter requirement
  (section 4.2), together with Krea's [Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.krea.ai/krea-2-use-policy).
- formcut installs the content filter that licence requires, Falconsai/nsfw_image_detection, with Krea 2 and runs it
  on every picture; there is no option to install Krea 2 without it. Because formcut is open source you can remove it,
  but that is your own, knowing decision: formcut warns you when the filter is gone, Krea 2 keeps running, and the
  responsibility for using Krea 2 without it, and for any breach of its licence that follows, is yours alone.

## What formcut will not do

- formcut refuses to generate sexual content involving minors, whether or not a content filter is installed. It is
  not a setting, and removing the content filter does not remove it.
